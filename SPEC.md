# SPEC — "Tomorrow's Morning, Today!" v3 (graphic poster, no characters)

```text
GOAL:    The viewer watches one circle turn a tired Monday into a Voltbean Monday, and
         leaves with: "One can and Monday doesn't stand a chance."
OUTPUT:  HyperFrames, 1920 x 1080, 30 fps
TIME:    30 s x 30 fps = 900 frames. Built on a 120 BPM grid (15 frames a beat) for a
         music bed dropped in later.
LOOK:    Mid-century modern poster design in motion (Paul Rand / Saul Bass era).
         - ground: flat colour fields, no gradients; paper grain tile at 55% multiply over all;
         - shapes: geometric only (circles, ellipses, rounded rects, polygons, strokes);
           NO characters, NO drawn people;
         - ink line drawn 12 px off register (-12x, -9y) from its fill;
         - BEFORE (dusty): rose #C79AA2, teal #8FA9A3, mustard #C9B84A, paper #E9E1D3,
           ink #2B2724;
         - AFTER (full): voltage #FFE600, chaos #FF1F7A, teal #1FB5A8, white #FAFAFA,
           ink #0A0A0A;
         Type: League Gothic 400 caps 64–330 px; Yellowtail 100–134 px for the
         announcer lines; small print League Gothic 34 px.
CARRIER: ONE CIRCLE through the whole film: clock face → coffee cup rim → the drained
         dot → the can's rivet and lid → (the can) → the banner shrinks to a dot → the
         end card's iris.
MOTION:  Arrivals expo.out / back.out(2) for stamps; exits power3.in at 40–50% of the
         entry; camera and floods power2/3.inOut; constant speed only where that is the
         point (second hand, heartbeat trace). Landed type never moves. Whips put the cut
         on the fastest frame.
SHOTS:   clock    0–4.5    sunburst clock, minute hand ticks to 7:04, "MONDAY." "7:04 A.M."
                           "Rise and… shine?"; spokes retract, wall floods teal.
         cup      4.5–9.5  the face becomes a cup rim from above; saucer, handle, weak
                           coffee, two sad ripples; "ORDINARY COFFEE." rolls to "MONDAY.";
                           the cup drains to one ink dot.
         can      9.5–15   the dot becomes a rivet, a lid grows round it; ring-pull lifts,
                           bolt escapes, yellow iris floods from the opening; the lid
                           flattens and the can turns to face us; "1000 MG!" starburst,
                           "Electrified cold brew."; zoom through the label's bolt to pink.
         effects  15–23    "SIDE EFFECTS MAY INCLUDE:" INBOX 247→0 as envelopes fly
                           (pink) → whip → calendar, Monday the 8th circled and ticked,
                           "MONDAY: HANDLED." (white) → whip → "HEARTBEAT: SURROUND
                           SOUND." with a trace (yellow) → whip → banner "Tomorrow's
                           morning, today!" (teal), which collapses to a dot.
         endcard  23–30    yellow iris from the dot; sun turns in; can rises; VOLTBEAN
                           painted on; "Coffee is dead." / "Long live VOLTAGE."; "Drink at
                           your own risk."; small print. Holds from 26.6 s.
BANNED:  drawn characters/people; dark void ground; glow, neon, particles; gradients;
         cream + serif + italic; everything centred; fade-and-slide-up; titles that
         drift; corner HUD; docked logo; glitch/RGB split.
```
